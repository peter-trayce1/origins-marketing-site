Solutions

Built for every kind of brand.

From independent labels to luxury houses and enterprise retailers, Known Objects scales to the size and complexity of your range.

Independent and sustainable brands

Prove your sustainability claims with verifiable data.

Structure your materials data, certifications, supply chain steps and impact metrics in a format that customers, stockists and regulators can check for themselves.

  • GOTS, OEKO-TEX, GRS and custom certification fields
  • Supply chain by country, facility and process
  • Impact metrics for carbon, water and waste
  • Customer-facing sustainability summaries

Premium and luxury brands

Protect provenance. Communicate craftsmanship.

Give every piece a branded passport covering provenance, materials, artisan stories and certifications, reachable by scanning the label.

  • Artisan and maker story fields
  • Provenance and origin documentation
  • Certification and authenticity fields
  • Anti-counterfeit QR identity

Scaling brands

Manage passports across your entire catalogue.

Whether the range is fifty products or five thousand, bulk import, Shopify sync and AI-assisted drafting keep a full passport library maintainable without a dedicated compliance team.

  • Bulk CSV import for large ranges
  • Shopify sync that drafts on new products
  • Templates by product category
  • Team workspace with role permissions

Ecommerce and DTC

Add passport badges, inline material summaries and full passport modals to any Shopify, Hydrogen or custom storefront, so the detail is there at the point of purchase.

Whatever your size
